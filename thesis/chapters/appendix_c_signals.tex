\subsection{Cross-market warning interpretation}

The per-pair results show that the warning layer behaves very differently from market to
market. The S\&P~500 produces the clearest warning episodes; NASDAQ is markedly weaker,
dipping below chance at the longer windows, probably because its sector concentration
adds idiosyncratic noise that the crypto channel cannot see. The metal and dollar targets
are noisier but not uniformly useless. The signal should therefore be applied selectively
rather than across the asset universe.
