# Sample questions

Good prompts for demoing Thesis Copilot. They exercise retrieval across the
results, methodology, and discussion chapters.

## Results / metrics
- Which model had the best RMSE, and why?
- What was the average RMSE and R-squared of Ridge regression?
- How did DCC-GARCH compare to the machine-learning models?
- Which asset pair was the easiest to forecast?

## Methodology
- What rolling-correlation window lengths were used?
- Why was the Fisher transformation applied to the target?
- What is walk-forward evaluation and why does it matter here?
- Which forecasting models were compared?

## Interpretation / discussion
- Why do simple persistence models outperform complex ones?
- What is the investor signal layer, and how well does it work?
- What are the main limitations of the thesis?
- Can an investor trade this model directly?

## Cross-lingual (multilingual embeddings)
- Why does DCC-GARCH perform worst? (English question, English thesis)
- Pochemu prostye modeli oboshli slozhnye? (Russian -> English thesis)
- Koji model ima najbolji RMSE? (Serbian -> English thesis)
